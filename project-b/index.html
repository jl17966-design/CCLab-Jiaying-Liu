What Remains?

What Remains is an interactive digital garden that explores the temporality of beauty. Using voice as the medium of creation, users generate roses that bloom on screen and the duration of their speech determines the flower's type and size. These flowers automatically fade after one minute of inactivity or without connecting by vines, which symbolize relationships requiring maintenance. The work asks a critical question: when the digital session ends and technology fades, what do we choose to preserve? Just as beauty vanishes without active care from each generation, this garden disappears when unattended.
